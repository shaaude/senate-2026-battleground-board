How to read this

FiveThirtyEight no longer publishes averages. ABC News shut 538 down in March 2025, so its slot here is filled by PollCheck’s weighted average plus this board’s own 14-day all-polls average (a plain mean of every poll on this page that ended or was released in the 14 days up to the newest one). No independent average was found for South Carolina.

RealClearPolitics is an unweighted mean of recent polls over the window shown on each card. Kalshi and Polymarket are traded prices for the Democrat winning, read as a percent chance; each card shows the date of the price used. Prices are not polls: they reflect traders’ money, and researchers caution they are not a direct read of voter sentiment.

Poll margins are Democrat minus Republican. “Party-linked” marks pollsters or sponsors with a declared lean (for example Trafalgar, GQR, Data for Progress). Some polls list only a release date. Every poll in the table links to where it was found.

Check for new data reloads the data files from this site. New data arrives when the scheduled research run commits it; the button does not run any research itself.
